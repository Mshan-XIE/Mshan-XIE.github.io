# Mingshan Xie

I am interested in the theoretical foundations of artificial intelligence, intelligent agents, and autonomous intelligence.

My current research focuses on understanding intelligent behavior in AI systems from theoretical, mathematical, and physics-inspired perspectives.

## Research Interests

- Theoretical Foundations of Artificial Intelligence
- Statistical Physics of Learning
- Intelligent Agents
